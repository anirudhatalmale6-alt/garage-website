====================================================================
GARAGE + USED CAR SALES WEBSITE — how it all works
====================================================================

Everything in the "public_html" folder goes into the public_html
folder of your HostGator account. That is the whole installation —
there is no database to create and nothing to configure.

The site needs PHP 7.4 or newer. HostGator gives you PHP 8 by
default, so there is nothing to change.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  mubautos.co.uk/                 Home
  mubautos.co.uk/cars.php         Used cars for sale
  mubautos.co.uk/car.php?id=...   One vehicle, with the photo gallery
  mubautos.co.uk/book.php         Book a service  <-- customers
  mubautos.co.uk/admin/           Stock Manager   <-- you only

The Stock Manager is linked in the footer under "Sales". If you would
rather it was not linked anywhere at all, say the word and I will take
the link out — the page still works, you just type the address in.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT YOU CAN DO YOURSELF
--------------------------------------------------------------------

VEHICLES  (Stock Manager -> Vehicles)

  * Add a vehicle       photos, specs, price, your own notes
  * Mark as SOLD        the red switch. The car greys out on the site
                        with a SOLD stamp across it, drops off the
                        home page and stops counting as available.
                        Leave it there — "have you got another one
                        like that?" is where a lot of calls come from.
  * Remove              deletes it from the website for good, and
                        deletes its photos off the server with it
  * The star            shows that car on the home page (pick 3)
  * The arrows          change the order cars appear in

  Photos go straight from your phone. Anything big is automatically
  shrunk so the page still loads quickly on 4G. Up to 30 per car.
  The first photo is the one people see in the listings — use "Make
  main" under any photo to promote it, or the arrows to shuffle the
  order of the gallery.

  If you are putting 20 or 30 photos on one car, add them in batches
  of about ten. It is quicker, and it will not time out halfway
  through on a slow connection. If a batch is too big for the server
  to swallow in one go the page now tells you so instead of quietly
  dropping them.

BOOKINGS  (Stock Manager -> Bookings)

  Every booking made on the website lands in the diary here, grouped
  by day, newest day first. For each one you get the customer's name,
  phone, email, what the car is, what they want doing and anything
  they typed in the notes.

  * Confirm    tells you at a glance which ones you have dealt with
  * Done       for after the job is finished
  * Cancel     puts the slot back on the website for somebody else
  * Delete     removes it from the diary for good

  The number in an amber circle next to "Bookings" in the menu is how
  many are still waiting for you to confirm them.

  IMPORTANT: the diary is the real record, not the email. A booking is
  written into the diary before any email goes out, so even if the
  mail server has a bad day the booking is never lost. If an email
  fails, the page says so at the top in red.

BOOKING SETTINGS  (Stock Manager -> Business details -> Online booking)

  * Let customers book online   untick it and the booking page turns
                                into a "give us a ring" page and the
                                Book Online link leaves the menu
  * Email new bookings to       set to mubautos@yahoo.com. Change it
                                to wherever you actually read email.
  * Slot length                 60 minutes to start with
  * Cars per slot               how many jobs you can start at once —
                                set it to how many ramps you can fill.
                                Set at 2.
  * Least notice                nobody can book inside the next 2
                                hours, so you are never ambushed
  * Book up to                  the calendar runs 42 days ahead
  * Lunch break                 leave blank for none
  * What people can book        the list in the dropdown, one per line
  * Days you are closed         bank holidays and your own days off,
                                one date per line as 2026-12-25

  The free slots on the calendar come straight out of your opening
  hours. Change your hours and the booking calendar changes with them
  — there is no second timetable to keep in step.

BUSINESS DETAILS  (Stock Manager -> Business details)

  Your name, logo, phone, mobile, WhatsApp number, address, opening
  hours, the words on the home page, your services, and the reviews.
  Change any of it and it changes everywhere on the site at once.

  Two things worth knowing:

  * The WhatsApp number needs the country code and no spaces.
    07123 456789  becomes  447123456789
  * The map and the Get Directions button follow your address. Type
    the address the way you would type it into Google Maps and both
    will point at the right place.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  public_html/data/stock.json      your vehicles
  public_html/data/settings.json   your business details
  public_html/data/bookings.json   your booking diary
  public_html/uploads/             your photos

That is your whole website's content. To back it up, download those
two folders. To move host, copy the whole public_html across — it
will come up exactly as you left it.

Nobody can read the data folder over the web; there is an .htaccess
in there blocking it, and a second rule in the main .htaccess as a
belt and braces.

6. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  "The Stock Manager says it cannot save"
        The data and uploads folders need to be writable. In cPanel's
        File Manager set both to permissions 755. If HostGator is
        fussy, 775 will do it.

  "I have forgotten the password"
        Message me. It is a one-line change to the settings file and
        takes a minute.

  "A photo will not upload"
        It is either not a JPG/PNG/WEBP, or the whole batch was too
        big for the server to take in one go. There is a .user.ini
        file in public_html that raises the limits to 40 files and
        220 MB a batch. If HostGator ignores it, just add the photos
        ten at a time — it works out the same.

  "A booking email did not arrive"
        Check the spam folder first. If it is genuinely not arriving,
        message me: the site sends from noreply@mubautos.co.uk using
        the hosting's own mail server, and sometimes that needs an SPF
        record adding at the DNS end. The bookings themselves are
        never affected — they are all in the diary either way.

Any question at all, message me on Freelancer and I will sort it.
